A Visit to the Library

Last Saturday, Riya visited the city library with her brother. The library was large, clean and quiet. There were many books about science, history, stories and geography. Riya selected a book about nature because she loves animals and plants. Her brother chose a book about space and planets. They sat at a table and read their books for two hours. Before leaving, they returned their old books and borrowed two new books. They enjoyed their visit and promised to come back next Saturday.

Answer the Questions

1. When did Riya visit the library?

2. Whom did Riya visit the library with?

3. Which book did Riya select?

4. How long did they read?

5. When did they promise to come back?
